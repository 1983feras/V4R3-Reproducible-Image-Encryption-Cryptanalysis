# Abstract Finalization Rule

The abstract currently contains only completed G1/G2/G3, session-transfer, and query-budget findings. It must remain conservative until the remaining confirmatory campaign completes.

Before submission, replace the pending-evidence sentence with exactly one evidence-driven outcome class:

- **Outcome S1:** V4-R3 survives implemented confirmatory attacks; V5-R3 shows no measurable benefit. Emphasize V4 and negative nonlinear ablation.
- **Outcome S2:** V4-R3 survives; V5-R3 produces a reproducible measurable reduction in attacker success. Report effect and uncertainty without mechanistic overclaim.
- **Outcome S3:** V4-R3 is reproducibly broken by a confirmatory attack. Make the break and its mechanism the principal result; do not redesign the cipher inside this paper's frozen campaign to hide the finding.

In all outcomes retain: empirical evidence is not a formal security proof; V4/V5 are experimental; standardized AEAD remains the appropriate production reference.
