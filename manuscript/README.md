# Manuscript Workspace

The manuscript is managed as an evidence-first workspace. Start with:

- `MANUSCRIPT_SKELETON.md` — full structural draft with completed evidence and explicit pending sections.
- `RESULTS_LEDGER.md` — authoritative completed/pending numerical evidence.
- `TITLE_AND_RQS.md` and `CONTRIBUTIONS.md` — frozen framing.
- `CLAIMS_MATRIX.md` — allowed versus prohibited inference.
- `STATISTICAL_ANALYSIS_PLAN.md` — frozen analysis rules.
- `TABLES_TEMPLATE.md` and `FIGURES_PLAN.md` — final presentation plan.
- `RESULTS_WORDING.md` — manuscript-ready wording for completed confirmatory results.
- `LIMITATIONS.md`, `REPRODUCIBILITY_STATEMENT.md`, `ETHICS_AND_SECURITY_SCOPE.md` — mandatory transparency sections.
- `REVIEWER_RESPONSE_PREMORTEM.md` — anticipated reviewer concerns.
- `FINAL_AUDIT_RULES.md` and `../protocol/FINALIZATION_CHECKLIST.md` — mandatory pre-submission audit.

Do not manually promote a development-only number into the manuscript. New results enter through the evidence ledger after execution and provenance verification.
