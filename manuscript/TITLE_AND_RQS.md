# Frozen Title and Research Questions

## Title
**Cryptanalysis-Guided Evaluation of Adaptive Image Encryption: Stateful Bidirectional Diffusion, Cross-Session Attacks, and Nonlinear Ablation**

## Research questions
- **RQ1:** Does the revised V4-R3 stateful bidirectional construction remove the previously observed position-dependent diffusion defect under frozen boundary-directed tests?
- **RQ2:** Under bounded known/chosen-plaintext diagnostics, can an attacker recover a practically useful reusable or equivalent transformation for V4-R3?
- **RQ3:** Does attack information learned under one key/nonce session transfer to an independent nonce or key/nonce session?
- **RQ4:** After cryptographic session derivation and stateful diffusion are already present, does the V5-R3 nonlinear extension provide a reproducible, measurable reduction in attacker success sufficient to justify its added complexity?

The answers are determined only from confirmatory evidence; no RQ has a preferred required outcome.
